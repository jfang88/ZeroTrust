Speaker notes: Top half is the NIST SP 800-207 core model. Every request from a subject goes through an enforcement point, which asks the decision point. The decision point weighs signals — identity strength, device health, threat intel, behaviour, data sensitivity — against policy and then opens or closes a session. Being "on the corporate network" earns nothing. Bottom band is the CISA ZTMM v2.0 view: five pillars that each mature at their own pace, held together by visibility, automation and governance. Key message: zero trust is an architecture and operating model, not a product — most of the pieces are things we already buy; the change is making them feed one policy decision.
