Enterprise resources

Apps, APIs, data and workloads, never directly reachable

Operating principles

  • Network location grants no trust
  • Least privilege, granted per session
  • Verify continuously, not once at login

Left is the NIST SP 800-207 core model. Every request from a subject goes through an enforcement point, which asks the decision point. The decision point weighs signals (identity strength, device health, threat intel, behaviour, data sensitivity) against policy and then opens or closes a session. Being "on the corporate network" earns nothing. Bottom band is the CISA ZTMM v2.0 view: five pillars that each mature at their own pace, held together by visibility, automation and governance. Key message: zero trust is an architecture and operating model, not a product. Most of the pieces are things we already buy; the change is making them feed one policy decision. That single decision point is also a new dependency, which is why resilience is designed in from day one (slide 5).
