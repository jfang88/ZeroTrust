This is a hypothesis, not an assessment. It reflects what most enterprises have when they start: good point products, weak integration. Read the middle column as "already paid for" and the right column as "what makes it zero trust". The pattern: identity and devices are partly there; network is usually the biggest structural change (VPN and flat internal networks); data and governance are the least mature. NSA's 2026 guidelines start with a Discovery phase for exactly this reason — you cannot write access policy for data, apps and flows you have not inventoried.
