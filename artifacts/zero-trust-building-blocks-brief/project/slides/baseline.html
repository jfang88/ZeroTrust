This is a hypothesis, not an assessment. It reflects what most enterprises have when they start: good point products, weak integration. Read the middle column as "already paid for" and the right column as "what makes it zero trust". Stages are on the CISA ZTMM scale so they carry straight into discovery scoring. The pattern: identity and devices are partly there; network is usually the biggest structural change (VPN and flat internal networks); data, non-human and supplier access, and governance are the least mature. We are not asking you to agree these scores, only that they are plausible enough to justify discovery.
